01 / The product

Everything you already know.

Forkalope keeps the workflow recognizable on purpose. Repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, packages, permissions, and automation should all feel like second nature.

The business model changes the relationship around it. You can run the software yourself, help fund shared infrastructure as a Co-Sysop, or pay Forkalope to run the service for your organization.

See the Co-Sysop plans →

02 / The network

Independent homes. Connected code.

One deployment can be useful. A constellation of them could be resilient, portable, and owned by the people running it.

  1. 01
    Fork the platform

    Clone the complete open-source project and configure it for your community or team.

  2. 02
    Sponsor a node

    Co-Sysops contribute one, two, or three Tines toward infrastructure that Forkalope operates and maintains.

  3. 03
    Connect when useful

    Independent nodes can share identity, discovery, and project relationships without sharing ownership.

03 / The model

Two ways to belong.

Co-Sysops help make the commons tangible. Businesses pay for the work of running it well.

For people Co-Sysop membership

$30, $60, or $90/month helps sponsor capacity, gives you a visible relationship to the network, and unlocks safe operator access.

Choose a Tine →
For organizations Managed Forkalope

Pay for hosting, backups, updates, migration, support, and operational accountability. The software remains free to deploy yourself.
