An operating role / proposed program

Become an Independent Sysop.

Rent a server in your own name, administer it yourself, and coordinate the Co-Sysops who help support your node. You hold the provider account, pay the infrastructure bill, and keep the administrative keys.

Joining the shared network would require an operator agreement and approval. It is a separate responsibility, not a higher-priced Tine plan. Installing the software alone does not enroll your machine or authorize it to receive customer data.

Your responsibilityRun a dependable node

Security, upgrades, backups, capacity, abuse response, and incident participation under published network requirements.

Your communityGive Co-Sysops a home

Coordinate stewardship and share appropriate node health information. Sponsorship never grants access to private customer repositories.

Your compensationGet paid for delivered service

Approved capacity and recovery work could earn an agreed service fee. Sponsorship allocations and billing would be set in the operator agreement, with no recruitment commissions.

The network we’re building

Someone else has room when your node needs help.

The proposed recovery network pairs approved operators, prepares authorized replicas, and reserves spare capacity. If a node fails, its recovery partner would help restore service while its operator repairs the original machine.

Recovery needs more than copied Git repositories: account state, permissions, other stored data, and routing must recover consistently too. We need tested procedures before enabling automatic failover or promising recovery times.

PrepareReplicas and spare capacity

Use approved locations and operators, with customer data permissions respected. Plan for provider failures as well as individual server failures.

PracticeTest the whole recovery

Measure restore time and possible data loss. Shared recovery is a planned capability, not a current availability guarantee.

The rules

No downlines. Only uplinks.

The proposed rules: no commissions for recruiting Co-Sysops or Sysops, no downlines, and no founder multipliers. The same published opportunity applies regardless of when you join or how many Tines you sponsor.

Separate partner commissions, paid bounties, migration work, support, and approved infrastructure work would compensate people for services actually delivered. Paid membership would not be required to apply. Rates and eligibility will be set before these programs launch.

Membership itself is not an investment or an entitlement to Forkalope revenue.
