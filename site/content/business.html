The pricing principle

Hire another developer.
Your Git bill doesn’t change.

Forkalope plans are designed around the service you use, not the number of people on your team. Unlimited members means your developers, designers, contractors, bots, and occasional collaborators can share one forge without a new seat charge for each account.

We still have to price the things that cost money to operate: repository and artifact storage, compute, bandwidth, CI runners, recovery replicas, retention, regions, and support. A larger bill should reflect more capacity or more service—not a head-count tax.

This is the pricing direction we’re taking into the business pilot. Exact limits and rates will be published before a paid plan opens.
