A separate lane

Businesses pay for service.

Co-Sysop dues support the commons. A company pays Forkalope for managed hosting, backups, updates, migration, and someone accountable when something goes wrong.

The software stays free to deploy yourself. Managed, dedicated, and bring-your-own-infrastructure options can grow around the same open platform.

The rules

No downlines. Only uplinks.

Co-Sysops never earn money by recruiting another Co-Sysop. Everyone starts at zero, and everyone follows the same published rules.

Separate partner commissions, paid bounties, migration work, and support work compensate people for real value they create for real customers or for the project. Membership itself is not an investment or an entitlement to Forkalope revenue.

Final membership, partner, and payout terms should be reviewed by counsel before cash programs launch.
